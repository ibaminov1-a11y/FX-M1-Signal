## Scope and architecture

Fast entry is selected explicitly by SCALP + entry M1. NORMAL and SCALP on other entry frames retain their existing paths. M5/M15 closed structural context selects a permitted direction, never an entry. A fresh observed M1-scale advance, pullback, resumption and micro-level crossing creates a standard ScenarioCore scenario and Decision. No new order sender, paid data, REAL permission, risk increase, forced mode/frame changes, or credential handling.

The existing Engine owns positions, order intents, stops, campaign budgets, reconciliation, pause, emergency and restart behavior. Candidate IDs are unique and consumed once. Original campaign target/invalidation remain auditable. Fast additions require a fresh sequence after each confirmed fill, favorable price, net profit, original source alive, room to target, risk and margin. All thresholds are explicit research assumptions, not performance claims.

## Review focus

- No historical crossing becomes an executable order after enabling/restart/feed recovery.
- Reading another timeframe cannot trade or alter selected profile.
- First confirmation can cross the frozen micro-level on the resumption tick without requiring a redundant later crossing.
- A failed/unknown order cannot be retried as a new event; no averaging losses or silent lot/risk increases.
- Context changes, expired sources, insufficient history and partial/missing fields cannot publish a ready stale setup.
